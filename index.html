1. Strip the watermark text

One phrase per line. Matching text is deleted from the file itself, so it stops being selectable, searchable and copyable. Scan first to see what would be hit.

If a hit is missed, the phrase probably wraps across two lines in that PDF. Add a shorter fragment such as hispanoteca.ru on its own line.

3. Clean up a scan

for scanned books that print gray

A scanned page is one big photo of the paper, so its "white" is really a light gray that the printer dutifully covers in ink. This measures the paper colour on each page image, turns it pure white, and darkens the print. The image is swapped in place: page size, layout and any text layer stay exactly as they were.

Photos and illustrations are left alone, as is any page that is real text rather than an image. Strong clears heavy shading near the spine but can thin light strokes. Undo and try another setting if the result looks wrong.

4. Safety check

for files downloaded from the internet

Looks for JavaScript, actions that run when the file opens, launch actions and embedded files, by walking every object in the document. Sanitising removes all of those and rebuilds the file.

Make a book from text

Paste the whole text. A line starting with # becomes a chapter title on a fresh page, ## a subheading, and *** on its own line a scene break. With detect chapter titles on, a line in CAPITALS, a roman numeral alone or Part One counts as a chapter too. Western European text only: the built-in fonts have no Cyrillic or Greek.
